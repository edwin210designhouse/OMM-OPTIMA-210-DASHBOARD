⚠ Report damages immediately — read before submitting

Check every item as soon as the container is unloaded and shaken out in the building. Report on the spot — do not set it aside to do later.

If something is damaged, lost, or wrong → fill this form and submit promptly. Anything reported more than a couple of days after unload cannot be claimed as transportation damage — by that point the damage is assumed to have happened during handling or installation, and it will not be covered.

Reports through Procore, Autodesk, or similar platforms come in during or after installation — those are installation reports, not delivery reports. Our contract ends at delivery, so by the time those come in the damage can no longer be traced back to transport.

📋 How to use this form: Fill in the fields for one item → click + Add to List. Repeat for each damaged item. When you've added everything → click ✉ Send Email. Your list is saved for this session only and clears when you close or refresh the page.

📷 Photos (desktop): Attach photos to your email and rename each one with the unit number and cabinet type (e.g. 6019_Base W31.jpg) so we can identify them. For multiple photos of the same item, add a number at the end (e.g. 6019_Base W31_2.jpg, 6019_Base W31_3.jpg).

📱 Photos (phone): If reporting on the spot from your phone, send one separate email per item with the photos for that damage attached (no need to rename).

📧 If Send Email doesn't open your mail app, click ⧉ Copy and paste the message into a new email to edwin@210designhouse.com.

📁 Install drawings can be found in the Shared Documents section of this dashboard.

🖨 Print Report covers container status only — damage and open items are not included in the printout.
